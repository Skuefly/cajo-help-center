Across every Cajo model

Cajo Avalon Carbon Angler Pro paddle weightThe Avalon Carbon Angler Pro paddle (230 to 260 cm adjustable) weighs 38 oz.Cajo glossaryA working glossary of the marine and kayak terms Cajo uses in spec sheets, product pages, and warranty conversations — defined plainly with one line on why each matters on a Cajo.Cajo Owner's CommunityThe Cajo Owner's Community lives in the Camino Journey Kayak Facebook group — that's where rigging photos, fishing trips, and on-water conversations actually happen. The video library is on @caminojourney.co on YouTube and TikTok. For warranty + claims + missing parts, go through the claims form (the FB group isn't a support queue). For pre-purchase questions, hit chat on caminojourney.com.Cajo standing decks: solid, no flexEvery kayak in the Cajo lineup has a solid, no-flex standing deck — we don't ship squishy decks.Caring for your CajoRinse and dry after every outing, keep rigid hulls out of the sun, store inflatables dry and loosely rolled, and use your Highlander regularly to prevent adhesive failure.Finding your HIN (hull serial number)Your HIN is etched into the hull itself — stern, starboard side, near the top edge — not printed on a sticker or label.Height range Cajo kayaks accommodateWith the Traverse Pedal Drive, comfortable use spans 4'6" to 7' tall. With a fin drive (Hobie MD 180 etc.), the range opens up to roughly 4' to 7.5' because fin drives add their own shaft-length adjustability.How to contact CajoThe fastest path is via chat on caminojourney.com or email — both hit our support inbox and create a trackable ticket. We're a remote support team, so we don't have a live phone number.Pedal drive: sold separatelyPedal drives (Traverse) and fin drive adapters are sold separately, with a $300 bundle discount whenever the Traverse Drive is purchased with any Cajo Highlander or kayak (applies on direct and dealer purchases — not exclusive to any one dealer).Safety on a CajoAlways wear a Coast Guard-approved PFD, mind the wind window, treat water under 50°F as a different sport with different gear, use USCG lighting at night, and take an ACA or NRS course to build real skills.Transporting a Cajo from home to the waterA standard kayak trailer handles the rigid Cajos; the Highlander AirTrek inflatables pack into a bag and ride in any vehicle; truck beds with foam blocks and a hitch extender work for the rigid line too.Usable weight capacity: what it meansUsable weight capacity is the self-bailing scupper limit — the maximum load at which the kayak still drains water out rather than pooling it in. It's a more conservative number than total float capacity, and it's the one Cajo publishes.What published width measuresPublished width is the maximum outside hull width, measured at the widest point near the center handles.

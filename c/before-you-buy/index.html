Before you buy

Choosing a boat, finding a dealer, and what things cost.

Best Cajo for a 6'5", 280 lb anglerThe Outpost 128 PDL is the best fit at that size — 500 lb usable capacity, 24" of seat travel, and room for paddlers up to 7' tall. The Terra 116 (475 lbs) is feasible but tighter on margin.Cajo auto-pilot and GPS-anchor: not yetCajo doesn't plan to develop its own motor or autopilot system — our focus is configurable hull designs that fit a wide range of third-party motor options.Cajo kayak availabilityCajo kayaks are shipping and available now. Rigid hulls are sold exclusively through authorized dealers; Highlander inflatables and accessories are available directly at caminojourney.com.Cajo video reviews in different conditionsOwner videos are starting to pop up — the Facebook Owner's Group at facebook.com/groups/caminojourneygroup is the best place for fresh reviews and rigged-setup photos. More official video content for the Outpost 100 and Terra 116 is on the way.Can you hunt from a Cajo kayak?Yes — waterfowl, bow, and even deer. Go with a rigid hull over an inflatable Highlander; the Terra 116's open deck is the standout, the Outpost 128 carries the heaviest rigs, and gun or bow mounts bolt to the gear tracks with no drilling.Content creator and pro staff discountWe don't offer a public discount in exchange for content. If you'd like to be considered for the Cajo creator / pro-team / ambassador roster, fill out our Content Field Team form.Custom hull orders: not offeredCajo doesn't build custom hulls — each mold costs $50,000–$100,000, so we put the engineering into configurable designs that adapt to many third-party motors and drives instead.Dealer application: phone number field errorOur application form doesn't accept some phone formats — email us your name, company, email, full shipping address, and phone number directly and we'll add you to the system manually.Demoing a Cajo kayak before buyingThe best path is to visit Electric Surf Sports in West Palm Beach, FL — they do on-water demos. Other dealers may demo as well, but we don't run a national demo tour at this time.Highlander 100 vs. 120 vs. 140TThe Highlander 100 is the small-water inflatable; the 120 gives you more room and capacity in the same packed footprint; the 140T is the tandem for two-person days.How to become a Cajo dealerAwesome — thanks for the interest! Fill out our dealer application form and our team will review and reach out within a few business days.How to choose your first Cajo kayakThree questions get you to the right Cajo — where you're fishing, how big you are, and whether you want rigid or inflatable.Inflatable vs. rigid Cajo: which to buyRigid Cajos (Terra / Outpost) are faster, stiffer, and easier to rig once; inflatable Cajos (Highlander AirTrek) pack to a trunk-sized bag and fish for real — pick based on where you store it, how you transport it, and how often you'll set up at the launch.Military discount on CajoWe don't have a direct military discount right now, but dealers who stock our kayaks may offer one — check with them.Outpost 100 vs. 128 vs. Terra 116The Outpost 100 is the lighter, smaller-water hull; the Outpost 128 is the big-water and tournament boat; the Terra 116 is the self-bailing, surf-and-salt option that sits in between.Pro team and affiliate programsPro staff, creator, ambassador, and affiliate inquiries all run through our Content Field Team form — we review every submission and reach out when there's a relevant opportunity.Quad Rails: launch and ship dateQuad Rails launched in stock in March 2026 — no pre-orders, they ship when they go live. Sign up for "Notify me when available" on the product page if a size is sold out.Traverse Drive bundle discountThe Traverse Pedal Drive is $300 off — $899.99 instead of $1,199.99 — when bundled with any Cajo hull, whether you buy direct or through an authorized dealer.Traverse Pedal Drive: use on a non-Cajo kayakThe Traverse Pedal Drive is engineered specifically for Cajo hulls and inflatables — it isn't supported on other manufacturers' kayaks.Treklite Kayak Seat: back-in-stock timingThe Treklite has limited supply ahead of full launch but is in stock and ready to ship now — if the product page says otherwise, contact support and we'll invoice you directly.Where Cajo kayaks are madeDesigned, engineered, and tested in-house. Manufacturing is handled by trusted overseas partners with strict QC and multi-stage inspection before shipping to dealers.Where to buy a CajoRigid hulls (Outpost, Terra) go through authorized dealers — find yours on the locator, or use Eco Fishing Shop (MO) or Electric Surf Sports (FL), which ship nationwide. Highlander inflatables and accessories ship direct from us.Where to find Cajo pricingAll current Cajo pricing lives on the product pages at caminojourney.com — that's the source of truth. The Traverse Pedal Drive carries a $300 bundle discount when purchased with any Cajo hull, applied automatically at checkout and honored by every authorized dealer.Will Cajo make its own fin driveCajo doesn't manufacture its own fin drive — instead we offer the Fin Drive Adapter, which works with the Hobie MD 180, Pelican, Vibe Hero 2.0–era, and Lightning Kayaks fin drives.

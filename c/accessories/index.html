Cajo accessory primerThe hull, seat, rudder, and foot braces come in the box; the decisions to make are your drive (Traverse vs. fin), Quad Rail sizing, second seat, electronics, and any use-case add-ons.Fish-finder transducer mount: Outpost 128The Outpost 128 has a removable electronics pod — transducer mounts to the bottom of the pod (no drilling required), and the top of the pod has a thru-hull wiring kit that runs cable and power into the pod without drilling the hull.Flat pedal-drive cover plateThe flat bolt-in drive mount plug isn't available for purchase yet — the plug your Terra ships with is the molded version, which does the same sealing job.Garmin foot pedal mount: Outpost fitDoable — we've seen Outposts running Garmin Force foot pedals — but there's no documented Cajo install spec, so ask in the Cajo Owner's Facebook group where owners who've rigged it can share what they did.Outpost 128 hardware & screw referenceThe Outpost 128 uses metric machine screws (M3–M6) into molded brass inserts, plus self-tapping screws (3.8–4.8mm) direct into plastic. Match the diameter and head type, use 316 stainless, and this table shows exactly what goes where.Slide Lock Foot Brace assembly manualThe Slide Lock Foot Brace toggles between a free-slide mode for rudder steering and a locked mode for standard bracing, with a rear knob for tuning cable tension and leg length.Spare parts and replacementsMost parts are available via live chat even though they aren't listed for browsing; the Spare Parts Kit is $99.99 and a spare Traverse prop is $29.99.Terra 116 Power Plates: thru-hull wiringThe Terra 116 has three factory-installed Power Plates — bow, cockpit, and stern — made to be drilled through when installing a thru-hull wiring kit, so you never drill the hull itself.

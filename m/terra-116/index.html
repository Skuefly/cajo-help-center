Specific to the Terra 116 PDL Pro

Answers that are only true of this one.

Terra 116 PDL specs and dimensionsThe Terra 116 PDL carries 475 lbs of usable weight capacity — the self-bailing scupper limit, so the kayak stays dry up to that load.Terra 116 Power Plates: thru-hull wiringThe Terra 116 has three factory-installed Power Plates — bow, cockpit, and stern — made to be drilled through when installing a thru-hull wiring kit, so you never drill the hull itself.Why does the Terra 116 arrive in more than one box?The Terra 116 ships in two boxes — the hull, and a separate parts box for the drive, rudder, seat, and hardware. Check for every box on arrival.Flat pedal-drive cover plateThe flat bolt-in drive mount plug isn't available for purchase yet — the plug your Terra ships with is the molded version, which does the same sealing job.

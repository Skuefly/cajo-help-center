Cajo kayak availability

Cajo kayaks are shipping and available now. Rigid hulls are sold exclusively through authorized dealers; Highlander inflatables and accessories are available directly at caminojourney.com.

Cajo kayaks are shipping and available now. Stock varies shop to shop, so the fastest answer for a specific model is your nearest dealer.

Rigid kayaks — through dealers

The Outpost and Terra are sold exclusively through authorized dealers; we don't ship rigid hulls direct to retail customers. The dealer locator is the source of truth for who's near you — call ahead to confirm the model you want is on their floor.

If there's no dealer in driving distance, two dealers ship Cajo hulls nationwide:

  • Eco Fishing Shop (Missouri) — ecofishingshop.com · 1-844-987-5777
  • Electric Surf Sports (West Palm Beach, FL) — electricsurfsports.com · (561) 501-3771

Highlander inflatables and accessories — direct from us

Highlander inflatables (100, 120, 140T) and our accessory line — Quad Rails, Horizon Perch, Treklite Seat, Fin Drive Adapter Kit — are available to order directly at caminojourney.com and ship parcel from our warehouse with tracking.

Replacement parts aren't listed on the site; message support with the part you need and we'll send an invoice.

If a model is out of stock

Sign up for "Notify me when available" on the product page and you'll get an email when it's back. For a rigid hull, your dealer is also worth a call — stock moves between shops faster than the site reflects.

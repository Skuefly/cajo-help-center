Treklite Kayak Seat: back-in-stock timing

The Treklite has limited supply ahead of full launch but is in stock and ready to ship now — if the product page says otherwise, contact support and we'll invoice you directly.

Short version: it's in stock and ready to ship now. The Treklite has limited supply ahead of full launch, so the product page can show as out-of-stock when we're between batches — but if you reach out, we can invoice you directly and ship from current inventory.

How to order if the page shows out of stock

  1. Contact support and let us know you want a Treklite — we'll confirm current inventory.
  2. Create an account at caminojourney.com with your shipping address and phone number so we have it on file for the invoice.
  3. We'll send a direct invoice via email. Once it's paid, the seat ships parcel from our warehouse.

This is the same flow we use for any direct accessory invoice — fast, no hoops.

Why it shows out of stock sometimes

Limited supply before full launch means the storefront inventory toggle flips off when a batch gets low, even if we still have units allocated for direct support invoices. We're aligning the storefront inventory more tightly as full-launch volume comes online — until then, the support route is the path of least resistance.

What's coming

Full Treklite launch and unrestricted storefront availability is later in 2026. Sign up for "Notify me when available" on the product page if you'd rather wait for full self-serve checkout.

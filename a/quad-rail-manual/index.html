Quad Rail Series assembly manual

The Cajo Quad Rail Series is an aluminum T-bolt gear-track rail — 12", 26", 32", and 44" (2-pack) — that installs on existing T-slot tracks or through-hull, with the full assembly walkthrough and PDF below.

The Cajo Quad Rail is your all-in-one mounting solution for limitless configuration. Whether you're building a captain's bridge, fishing dashboard, or streamlined handrails, the Quad Rail delivers 360° rigging potential with T-bolt gear tracks on all four sides. Each aluminum rail stands 3" tall to keep your gear elevated, secure, and ready in rough water. A built-in ruler adds quick measuring utility, and adjustable mounting posts ensure it fits nearly any kayak whether you're using gear tracks or the included through-hull hardware.

This article is the structured summary of the full Cajo Quad Rail Series Assembly Manual. Download the printable PDF below.

Features

  • T-bolt compatible gear tracks on all 4 sides
  • Available in 12", 26", 32", and 44" (2-pack) lengths
  • Mount parallel to the gunwale or across the hull
  • 3" height keeps gear elevated and secure
  • Built-in ruler for quick measurements
  • Adjustable mounting posts for maximum compatibility
  • Doubles as sturdy handrails for added control and safety
  • 26" and 32" ideal for captain's bridge or dashboard builds
  • 44" 2-pack gives full-length mounting flexibility

What ships in the box

Major components:

  1. Quad Rail (1) — the aluminum extrusion
  2. Mounting posts (2) ×2

Hardware kit:

  • A — Track nuts ×4
  • B — M6×22 bolts ×4 (plus 1 Allen key)
  • C — Backing plates ×2
  • D — M6 nuts ×2
  • F — Combination wrench

Assembly walkthrough (3 steps)

Step 1 — Partially tighten the mounting posts

Partially tighten the mounting posts to the Quad Rail using the provided bolt (part B) and track nut (part A). Keep it loose enough to slide the brackets along the track to your ideal position.

Step 2 — Adjust position and slide

Adjust the mounting posts to your ideal position before tightening.

Step 3 — Mount to hull

Two installation cases — pick yours.

Case 1: Mounting to existing T-slot gear tracks Mount the support brackets to the T-slot gear track using the provided bolt (part B) and track nut (part A). After mounting the support brackets to the gear track, fully tighten the top of the support bracket to the Quad Rail.

> Note: Some Cajo kayaks include brass inserts for Quad Rail mounting. If your kayak has these inserts, use only the mounting bolt (Part B) to fasten the bracket directly to the hull — no track nut required.

Case 2: Through-hull mounting

  1. After determining your mounting location, drill a hole through the hull using an M6 drill bit.
  2. Use the provided backing plate (part C) to reinforce your Quad Rail installation.
  3. Insert the bolt (part B) through the mounting bracket and the hull.
  4. Place the backing plate inside the hull and secure it with the mounting nut (part D).
  5. Use silicone sealant for waterproofing.

Picking the right length

  • 12" — single-accessory mounts (rod holder, camera arm, fish finder)
  • 26" — captain's bridge for cockpit-level instruments
  • 32" — extended dashboard for multi-instrument or wider builds
  • 44" (2-pack) — full-length parallel rails for maximum coverage

Content creator and pro staff discount

We don't offer a public discount in exchange for content. If you'd like to be considered for the Cajo creator / pro-team / ambassador roster, fill out our Content Field Team form.

We review every submission and reach out when there's a relevant opportunity.

Why no public creator discount

The honest version: a blanket "we'll give you 30% off if you post about it" program tends to attract the wrong fit. We'd rather work with creators we've actually evaluated — paddlers, anglers, content folks whose audience and approach match what we're building — than run a public deal that generates a lot of low-engagement posts.

So instead of a public discount, we run the Content Field Team as a curated roster. If there's a fit, the relationship comes with whatever support actually makes sense — gear, content collaboration, event invites, pro-deal pricing on additional purchases, etc.

What goes into a strong submission

  • Consistent content output in paddlesports, fishing, or adjacent outdoor verticals
  • Honest takes on gear — including stuff we don't make
  • Specifics on what you'd want to do with Cajo — long-form review, build series, tournament coverage, instructional, etc.
  • Engagement, not just follower count

What happens after you submit

If there's a fit, you'll hear from our team. We don't reply to every submission individually — there are a lot — but every submission goes into the roster we pull from for opportunities. Not hearing back right away isn't a no forever; it's a "we'll keep you on file."

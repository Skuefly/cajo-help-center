Military discount on Cajo

We don't have a direct military discount right now, but dealers who stock our kayaks may offer one — check with them.

We don't have a direct military discount at this time. But dealers who stock our kayaks may offer one — check with them.

Why direct vs. dealer matters

Dealer pricing terms are set by each dealer. A handful of paddlesports and outdoor retailers run their own military / veteran / first-responder discount programs on the brands they carry — that's a dealer-level decision, not something we set at the brand level.

If you're shopping a Cajo and you qualify for a military discount, ask the dealer. A few questions worth getting on the phone:

  • "Do you have a military / veteran / first-responder discount?"
  • "Does it stack with the $300 Traverse Drive bundle?"
  • "Do I need to show ID or apply through ID.me / Sheer ID?"

Dealers like Eco Fishing Shop, Electric Surf Sports, and others may have programs in place — easy to ask.

What we do offer

  • The $300 Traverse Drive bundle discount — applies automatically when you bundle the drive with a hull, on direct purchases AND dealer orders. See the $300 Traverse Drive bundle.
  • No surprise pricing at the dealer — same MSRP and same bundle math everywhere.

If this changes

If we add a direct military discount program in the future, it'll be announced on the storefront. Right now it's a "check with the dealer" answer.

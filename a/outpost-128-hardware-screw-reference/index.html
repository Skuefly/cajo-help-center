Outpost 128 hardware & screw reference

The Outpost 128 uses metric machine screws (M3–M6) into molded brass inserts, plus self-tapping screws (3.8–4.8mm) direct into plastic. Match the diameter and head type, use 316 stainless, and this table shows exactly what goes where.

Everything on the Outpost 128 mounts one of two ways: metric machine screws threaded into brass inserts molded into the hull, or self-tapping screws driven straight into the plastic. This page lists what goes where so you can replace a lost screw with the exact match.

Two rules for any replacement:

  • Use 316 stainless steel. These boats fish saltwater; 316 resists corrosion where 304 and zinc won't.
  • Match the diameter, head type, and length. An M5 countersunk (flat) screw and an M5 mushroom (truss) screw are the same thread but seat completely differently — bring the old one or check the table below.

The molded inserts

The brass inserts are metric, in four diameters — M3, M4, M5, and M6 (M5 is by far the most common on the boat). They take standard metric machine screws: match the diameter and use a standard coarse-thread screw of that size (pitch is standard coarse — M3 = 0.5 mm, M4 = 0.7, M5 = 0.8, M6 = 1.0).

Thread size by mounting point:

  • Power-Pole mount (stern) — M6
  • Bow trolling-motor mount — M6; the quick-release uses M6 × 1.0
  • Traverse rudder base, brush guards, and drive mount — M5
  • Most small fittings (drain plug, J-hooks, skid plates) — M5
  • Steering rope guides — M4

Horizon Pro Seat assembly manual

Assembly manual for the Horizon Pro Seat — an 8-position, high-low kayak seat with gear-track armrests, a removable dropbox, lumbar support, and a 350 lb capacity — with a 6-step walkthrough and PDF download below.

The throne of kayak seats. The Horizon Pro Seat is built for serious kayak anglers who demand more than just a place to sit. 8 adjustable seating positions (two heights × four recline angles), high-low functionality, breathable mesh fabric — designed for comfort, control, and customization. Integrated armrests feature gear tracks for easy rigging, while the removable under-seat storage bin includes a zippered PVC pouch. Lumbar support and the optional Horizon Elevated Seat Perch keep you locked in for the long haul.

This article is the structured summary of the full Horizon Pro Kayak Seat Assembly Manual. Download the printable PDF below.

Features

  • 8 seating positions for custom comfort (two heights × four recline angles)
  • High-low adjustable seating platform
  • Armrests with integrated gear tracks
  • Breathable, water-wicking fabric
  • Lumbar support for all-day fishing
  • Removable under-seat storage bin (Dropbox) with PVC zipper pouch
  • Fits Horizon Elevated Perch Seat
  • 350 lb weight capacity

Assembly walkthrough (6 steps)

Step 1 — Seat base frame

Connect the seat base tie rods (1) between the seat base supports (left + right) using M5×14 bolts (part A, ×4). This forms the base rectangle.

Step 2 — Attach the bottom frame + left armrest

Attach the bottom frame (4) with the left armrest (5) and left armrest backing plate (6) using M6×35 bolts (part D, ×4) and M6×20 bolts (part C, ×3).

Step 3 — Attach the right armrest

Attach the right armrest (7) and right armrest backing plate (8) using M6×35 bolts (part D, ×4) and M6×20 bolts (part C, ×3).

Step 4 — Install seat bottom

Insert the seat bottom (9) into the seat frame using M6×40 bolts (part E, ×4) and M6 nuts (part F, ×4) — 2 on each side at the hinge points.

Step 5 — Install backrest + attach to bottom strap

Place the seat backrest (10) onto the backrest hinge brackets. Use M5 nuts (part B, ×2) and M6×20 bolts (part G, ×2) to attach the seat bottom strap to the strap buckle on the backrest.

Step 6 — Install onto seat base + add Dropbox

Place the Horizon Pro Seat onto the seat base. Engage the yellow Mantis Seat Clips until they click to ensure the seat is properly secured.

Slide the Dropbox (11) under the seat until it engages the rear stoppers. Use the bungee lashings on the front of the Dropbox to secure the bin in place.

User guide — 8 functions

  1. Backrest Straps — Tighten or loosen the straps to recline or raise the seat back.
  2. Recumbent Angle Selector — Take weight off the seat. Pull the seat base forward to pull it out of the bracket. Align both sides and slide the base into any of the four bracket positions. Make sure both brackets are fully seated before sitting.
  3. Armrest Gear Tracks — Use the armrest gear tracks to mount steering handles and standard T-bolt accessories.
  4. Mantis Seat Clips — Engage all 4 seat clips until they snap into place to secure the Horizon Pro Seat to the seat base.
  5. Horizon Seat Removable Dropbox — Use the bungee tabs on the ends of the Dropbox to secure the bin under the seat.
  6. Seat Base Quad Rails — Use the under-seat Quad Rails to mount accessories and gear.
  7. Seat Base Trim Adjustment Thumbscrew — Loosen thumbscrews to adjust seat base forwards and backwards. Tighten the thumbscrews when the seat base is in the ideal position.
  8. High-Low Position — Disengage all 4 Mantis Seat Clips to move the seat from the high position to the low position. Re-engage the seat clips until they click to ensure the seat is secured before using.

Seat angle adjustment

  1. Pull the seat base forward until both supporting posts disengage from the handrails.
  2. Lift or lower the seat base to the desired position and insert the supporting posts to the corresponding slots.
  3. Push the seat base inward until both supporting posts snap securely into the handrail slots.
  4. The seat is now at your desired angle.

Seat height adjustment (high → low)

  1. Pull all four yellow Mantis Seat Clips outward to unlock them from the seat base.
  2. Lift the seat up.
  3. Lower the seat at an angle and ensure the rear seat base is securely locked into the lower slot.
  4. Close the front two yellow Mantis Seat Clips to secure the seat in the low position.

Handrail gear tracks

The handrail T-slot gear tracks can be used to mount:

  • Traverse Rudder Steering Handle Mount (see Traverse Rudder System manual)
  • Other T-bolt accessories

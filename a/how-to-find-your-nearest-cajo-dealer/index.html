How to find your nearest Cajo dealer

Use the dealer locator at caminojourney.com/pages/dealer-locator and call ahead to confirm stock — if there's no dealer near you, two authorized dealers ship Cajo hulls nationwide.

Cajo hulls are sold through authorized dealers — we don't ship boats direct from caminojourney.com. The dealer locator is the front door. Here's how to use it, what to ask the dealer before you drive over, and what to do if there's no dealer near you.

Step 1 — The dealer locator

Go to caminojourney.com/pages/dealer-locator. Enter your zip code or city, and the locator returns authorized dealers ordered by distance.

Step 2 — Call ahead

It's worth a quick call before you make the drive. Ask whether the model you want is on the floor, and in the colorway you're after — dealers don't always stock every model and color. If demos matter to you, ask about those too: some dealers run demo days, others will let you sit in the boat in the showroom.

If they don't have your boat in stock, ask how quickly they can order one in. And if you're planning to add the Traverse Pedal Drive, mention the $300 bundle discount — it applies at any authorized dealer, but it doesn't always show up in a quote unless you ask.

Step 3 — If there's no local dealer

Two of our authorized dealers ship Cajo hulls nationwide:

  • Eco Fishing Shops — ecofishingshop.com · 1-844-987-5777
  • Electric Surf Sports — electricsurfsports.com · (561) 501-3771

Both handle freight LTL to your home address — the same boat at the same price from an authorized source, with full warranty coverage.

If you'd specifically rather wait for a local dealer to open in your area, we have ongoing dealer recruitment in most US states. Drop us a chat with your zip and we'll let you know if anything's pending.

What if I want to become a dealer?

Fill out the dealer application at caminojourney.com/pages/dealer-application. We review every submission. If you own a kayak shop or are interested in carrying Cajo, that's where it starts.

Where to buy a Cajo

Rigid hulls (Outpost, Terra) go through authorized dealers — find yours on the locator, or use Eco Fishing Shop (MO) or Electric Surf Sports (FL), which ship nationwide. Highlander inflatables and accessories ship direct from us.

It depends which boat you want. The rigid hulls — Outpost 100, Outpost 128, and Terra 116 — are sold through authorized dealers in the US. The Highlander AirTrek inflatables (100, 120, 140T) ship direct from us, as do accessories like Quad Rails, the Horizon Perch, the Treklite Seat, the Traverse Pedal Drive, and the Fin Drive Adapter Kit, all parcel from our Tennessee warehouse. Start at the dealer locator, call ahead to confirm stock, and if nobody's close, two dealers ship nationwide.

How to find your nearest dealer

  1. Go to the locator — caminojourney.com/pages/dealer-locator. Enter your zip or city and it returns authorized dealers ordered by distance.
  2. Call ahead before you drive. Inventory varies by dealer and by week — confirm they have your model (Outpost 100, Outpost 128, Terra 116, or Highlander 100/120/140T) and color on the floor. Ask about demos too — some dealers run demo days, others will let you sit in the boat in the showroom. A 5-minute call saves a 2-hour drive.
  3. Mention the Traverse Pedal Drive bundle. If you're adding the Traverse Pedal Drive, ask about the $300 bundle discount — it applies at any authorized dealer but doesn't always show up in a quote unless you ask.
  4. No local dealer? Two authorized dealers ship nationwide (below), or drop us a chat with your zip — dealer recruitment is ongoing in most states and we can tell you if anything's pending.

The two nationwide shippers

If there's nothing in driving range, Eco Fishing Shop (Missouri) and Electric Surf Sports (West Palm Beach, FL) both carry full Cajo inventory year-round and freight hulls to any address in the contiguous US — same boat, same price, full warranty coverage. East of the Mississippi both have decent transit times; west coast and mountain west customers are still better off with one of these two than going without, even with a longer freight run. For Arkansas, Texas, and the Great Lakes states, Eco Fishing Shop tends to be the shorter freight origin; for coastal South Carolina and Florida, Electric Surf Sports usually is.

Ordering direct — what you can and can't buy from us

  • Highlander AirTrek inflatables ship direct. The 100, 120 and 140T go through standard checkout at caminojourney.com — no dealer needed, in the US and to Canada.
  • Accessories ship direct, parcel via FedEx from our Tennessee warehouse: Quad Rails, Horizon Perch, Treklite Seat, Traverse Pedal Drive, Fin Drive Adapter Kit. Add to cart at caminojourney.com and check out.
  • Replacement parts and hardware aren't listed for browsing — message support on live chat with your model and the part, and we'll invoice you. It ships as soon as that's paid.
  • Rigid hulls (Outpost, Terra) are dealer-only in the US. They're in stock and shipping now, so there's nothing to pre-order — start with a local dealer if you have one (lower freight, see the boat in person, local after-sale support), or one of the two nationwide shippers above.
  • Why are the rigid hulls dealer-only? Dealers handle freight, local delivery, demos, and after-sale support better than a single-warehouse DTC model could — it's a deliberate choice, not a missing feature. The inflatables pack into a bag and ship parcel, which is why they don't need that.
  • Outside the US, if there's no local distributor in your country, we ship direct via LCL — see below.

Ordering from outside the US (no local distributor)

  1. Create an account at caminojourney.com with your shipping address and phone number.
  2. Tell us what you want — kayak, drive, accessories — and we'll send a direct invoice.
  3. International shipping: $300 USD flat.
  4. We crate everything for shipping protection and ship via LCL (Less than Container Load). Average EU delivery: ~50 days. Other regions can run longer — you're paying for consolidation time at origin, ocean transit, and customs clearance, not a dedicated container.

This LCL flow covers the EU, Canada (rigid hulls only), Australia, Mexico, and Panama. It's the exception to our dealers-only model, used only where there's no local distributor — as regional coverage expands, the direct flow phases out in favor of local dealer purchases.
